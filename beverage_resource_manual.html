Our Barista Promise

The quality contract that keeps every customer coming back.

Love your beverage or let us know. We’ll always make it right.

— Our Barista Promise · FY25 Beverage Resource Manual
What the Promise Means
Our Barista Promise is our quality contract with our customers. It gives them permission to try something new, without risk. YOU are the reason our customers return — it is your ability to make every moment right that keeps them coming back.
Service Recovery — The L.A.T.T.E. Model
L
Listen
▼
Listen without interrupting. Let the customer fully express their emotions. Do not jump to solutions — they need to feel heard first. This is the most important step.
A
Apologize
▼
Apologize without condition — never make excuses or place blame. “I’m sorry that happened” is always appropriate. An unconditional apology acknowledges the customer’s experience.
T
Take Action
▼
Take action quickly. Remake the beverage, offer a replacement. Speed of recovery matters — a fast resolution often leaves the customer more satisfied than if nothing had gone wrong.
T
Thank
▼
Thank the customer for giving us an opportunity to make it right. They trusted us enough to speak up — that deserves genuine gratitude.
E
Ensure Satisfaction
▼
Watch customer cues after taking action. If still not satisfied, ask: “What can I do to make this right for you?” Don’t assume — confirm it.
Sampling Standards
1

Prepare According to Recipe

Always prepare sampling beverages to standard — never take shortcuts with samples.

2

Serve in 4 fl oz Sample Cups

Divide into sample cups and complete the beverage if applicable. Share at the hand-off plane or Drive Thru window.

3

Give Beverage Name & Description

Always state the beverage name as you hand it out — especially critical for samples containing potential allergens (soy, almond, coconut).

4

Never Serve Directly to Children

Samples must be handed off to a parent or guardian. All stores must have coffee presses ready to sample brewed coffee.

Starbucks Delivers — Key Guidelines
🔥
Steam All Milk-Based Hot Beverages Extra Hot
▼
For delivery orders, always steam milk-based hot beverages extra hot so they arrive at an acceptable temperature for the customer.
🥶
Hot & Cold Beverages in Separate Bags
▼
Always keep hot and cold beverages in different bags to maintain temperature integrity. Use Hot Delivery Lids for all hot beverages. Do not top hot beverages with whipped cream unless the customer specifically requested it.
📦
Seal All Food & Beverages
▼
Seal all food and beverages in their respective bags. Once sealed, the bag should not be opened until received by the customer.

Customization & Charging

Acknowledge that the beverage is not on our regular menu and let the customer know we have many ways to customize beverages — so you can help them find what they are looking for.

— FY25 Beverage Resource Manual
💰
What Customers Are Charged For
▼
Each additional espresso shot above standard recipe · Each additional Frappuccino® roast pump above standard · Plant-based milk substitution · Alternative milk and heavy cream as creamers (per 2 oz, up to 4 oz) · Adding more than 118 ml whole milk, nonfat or breve to water-based beverages.
✓
When Extras Are NOT Charged
▼
Syrups, sauces, or dry inclusions already in the recipe are not charged as long as the additional amount does not exceed 2× the original recipe quantity. Mocha drizzle, whipped cream, and chai concentrate already in the recipe are not charged for the standard addition.
🍉
Sweetness Variation Requests
▼
Less Sweet (Sugar Cane Syrup beverages): Reduce Sugar Cane syrup by 1 pump. Cup mark: V/LS.

Not Sweet: Zero pumps of Sugar Cane Syrup. Cup mark: V/NS.

Frappuccino® Blended: Less Sweet = use Light Frappuccino® Drink Bases (e.g., CFL, MFL).

Shot Timing Guidelines
⏱
Mastrena® II — Shot Timing
▼
Pull five sets of double shots. Look for the star symbol to indicate shots are within standard. If outside standard, pull five more double shots. If the problem persists, consult the Store Cleanliness and Equipment Maintenance Manual.
⏱
La Marzocco — Shot Timing (18–23 seconds)
▼
Time shots every 15 minutes. A good shot looks like “honey dripping from a spoon” with 3 clear visible layers.

Fast pour (<18 sec): Grind too coarse / tamp too low → adjust grind finer.

Slow pour (>23 sec): Grind too fine / tamp too high → adjust grind coarser.

Quality Check — 3× Daily Minimum
Partners must verify espresso quality three times daily, minimum — before peak, mid-shift, and after peak. All partners on shift must taste espresso once per shift. Taste from a demitasse cup — not from the shot glass.

Critical Standard — Never Re-Steam
Do not add fresh milk to steamed milk. A fresh pitcher must be steamed for each beverage. Milk is never re-steamed. Steamed milk that falls below 66°C (150°F) must be discarded immediately.

Non-Dairy Guidelines
🌿
Oatmilk
▼
Not a creamer — never give out as a creamer. Pre-chill 4 hours prior to use. Once opened, keep refrigerated (5 days). Oatmilk separates — shake well before each use. Aerate 1–3 seconds for consistent foam.
🍃
Coconutmilk
▼
Not a creamer — never give out as a creamer. Pre-chill 4 hours. Once opened, refrigerate (4 days OraRi / 7 days SunOpta). Coconutmilk separates — shake well before each use. Aerate 1–3 seconds.
🫘
Soymilk
▼
Pre-chill 4 hours prior to use. Once opened, refrigerate (3 days). Aerate 1–3 seconds for consistent foam.
🏷
Alternative Milk Stickers
▼
Use dedicated alternative milk stickers (Soymilk, Coconutmilk, Oatmilk) on cups with alternative milk orders to ensure accuracy at hand-off.

Cold Brew Procedure (Toddy Brewer)
1

Prep & Grind

Wash hands and put on food service gloves. Open the filter, folding the top 5 cm (2 in.) over, and place into the dry Toddy Brewer. Fill with ground Cold Brew Blend per recipe.

2

Fill

Pour 7L (full batch) or 4.5L (half batch) of cold filtered water into the filter, ensuring all grounds are wet. Tie the filter string in a knot, leaving 6–10 cm (3–4 in.) at the top.

3

Brew

Pour remaining water on top of the closed filter. Cover with lid. Date code. Brew unrefrigerated for 20 hours. Do NOT move the Toddy Brewer — agitation causes over-extraction and impacts quality.

4

Dispense

Transfer concentrate into plastic 2L pitchers or storage cubes. Label: 7 days refrigerated. Dilution ratio: 1:1 concentrate to cold water.

5

Wash & Sanitize

Always wash and sanitize the Toddy Brewer before the next batch. Refer to the Cold Brew Toddy Brewer Equipment Maintenance Manual.

Tea Steeping Times — Tap a Type

White Tea — 3 Minutes at 80°C / 176°F

Varieties: Youthberry Tea

The least processed of all teas. Steep for 3 minutes. Sachets per size: Tall = 1, Grande = 1, Venti = 2.

Green Tea — 3 Minutes at 80°C / 176°F

Varieties: Emperor’s Clouds & Mist, Mint Citrus Tea

Minimally oxidized. Steep for 3 minutes. Sachets: Tall = 1, Grande = 1, Venti = 2.

Oolong Tea — 7 Minutes at 80°C / 176°F

Varieties: Zen Clouds Oolong Tea

Partially oxidized — sits between green and black tea. Longest steep time at 7 minutes. Sachets: Tall = 1, Grande = 1, Venti = 2.

Black Tea — 5 Minutes at 80°C / 176°F

Varieties: Black Tea, Golden Monkey Tea, English Breakfast Tea, Earl Grey Tea

Fully oxidized — the most robust tea type. Brew for 5 minutes. Sachets: Tall = 1, Grande = 1, Venti = 2.

Herbal Tea — 5 Minutes at 80°C / 176°F

Varieties: Hibiscus Tea, Chamomile Tea, Mint Blend Tea

Tisanes from botanicals — not from Camellia sinensis leaves. Steep for 5 minutes. Sachets: Tall = 1, Grande = 1, Venti = 2.
